Statistics concepts

Fifteen distinct ways to understand recorded activity, data quality, and proposed planning signals. Concepts 06–15 explore unshipped modules that require new data or APIs.

  1. 01

    Executive narrative

    Period performance told as a guided financial story.

  2. 02

    Analyst workstation

    Dense controls, comparison KPIs, chart, and breakdown in one view.

  3. 03

    Question-led tabs

    Analysis organized around questions instead of chart types.

  4. 04

    Comparison board

    Current and prior periods shown side by side for fast deltas.

  5. 05

    Category-first explorer

    Expandable taxonomy drives the analysis and trend context.

  6. 06

    Spending health

    Transparent component signals explain a non-judgmental summary score.

  7. 07

    Anomaly radar

    Evidence-led review inbox for potentially unusual activity.

  8. 08

    Cashflow forecast

    90-day balance range with known events and uncertainty.

  9. 09

    Recurring commitments

    Subscription inventory, price changes, and renewal timeline.

  10. 10

    Goal projections

    Illustrative goal paths based on selected monthly contributions.

  11. 11

    Merchant patterns

    Merchant frequency, ticket size, categories, and recurrence evidence.

  12. 12

    Year-end review

    An export-ready annual story with changes and milestones.

  13. 13

    Scenario lab

    Static what-if controls with baseline and illustrative outcome.

  14. 14

    Cashflow calendar

    Income, bills, and projected balances arranged by day.

  15. 15

    Data quality center

    Coverage checks and an actionable cleanup queue.

All controls are static and illustrative. Concepts 01–05 reframe shipped workflows; 06–15 are proposals requiring new product capabilities. See the comparison matrix.
