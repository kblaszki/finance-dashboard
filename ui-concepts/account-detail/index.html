Account detail concepts

Five account workspaces for understanding balance history, editing ledger rows and safely importing statements.

  1. 01

    Ledger workstation

    A table-first working surface for fast cash-ledger maintenance.

  2. 02

    Balance story

    A chart-led narrative connecting balance changes to important activity.

  3. 03

    Statement view

    A familiar monthly statement with grouped daily entries and totals.

  4. 04

    Activity timeline

    Chronological activity with transfer context and a focused editor.

  5. 05

    Reconciliation cockpit

    A complex import mapping and duplicate-review workflow.
